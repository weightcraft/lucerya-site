## Common questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Lucerya → Cancel Subscription.

**How do I restore my subscription on a new device?**
Open Lucerya → on the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchase."

**How do I delete my data?**
Open Lucerya → Settings → Data → Clear All Data.

**How do I export my data?**
Open Lucerya → Settings → Data → Export.

**Is my data shared with anyone?**
No. All data stays on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](/lucerya-site/privacy).
